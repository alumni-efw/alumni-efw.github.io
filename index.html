How to join

Start in the way that works for you

You can request access to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or both. An Alumni admin will review your request and may message you privately if we need to confirm your English for Work connection.

You do not need to attend every event, answer every message, or be active all the time. You can read updates, join Talk Time or other events when you have time, connect with Alumni, share an idea, or come back later when you need support.

The people behind Alumni

A community shaped by its members

EFW Alumni Community is supported by a volunteer Leadership Team and Alumni members. The Leadership Team helps organize activities, welcome new members, support communication channels, update resources, and keep the community useful.

Every member also helps the community grow in their own way.
