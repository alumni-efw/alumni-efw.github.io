How it works

What happens at Talk Time

Every session follows a simple, welcoming structure so you always know what to expect.

Step 01

Topic introduction

The facilitator opens with a short intro to the week's topic, sharing discussion questions, examples, and useful vocabulary to help everyone feel ready.

Step 02

Breakout conversations

You move into small Zoom breakout rooms of 2-3 people. You can respond to the questions, share a story from your life, or simply listen - whatever feels right for you.

Step 03

Share and close

Everyone returns to the main room for a short closing. Participants share one thought, question, or takeaway from the conversation before we wrap up.

Get involved

Want to facilitate Talk Time?

You do not need to be a professional teacher. As a facilitator, you help create a friendly space where Alumni members can speak, listen, and practice without pressure.

You can lead one time or on a regular basis - using ready-made materials from the Leadership Team or your own topic.
