Keep practicing English with your community

Talk Time is a friendly online meeting where Alumni members practice English, share experiences, and support each other. No preparation needed - just show up and join the conversation.

Safe space to practice

Speak freely in a low-pressure, supportive environment with peers who understand your journey.

One topic, two chances to join

You can join one or both weekly sessions. The current time and Zoom link are shared in WhatsApp *.

Small-group conversations

Breakout rooms of 2-3 people give you real speaking time, not just listening time.

* New to Alumni? Start with Ways to Connect to request access.

How it works

What happens at Talk Time

Every session follows a simple, welcoming structure so you always know what to expect.

Step 01

Topic introduction

The facilitator opens with a short intro to the week's topic, sharing discussion questions, examples, and useful vocabulary to help everyone feel ready.

Step 02

Breakout conversations

You move into small Zoom breakout rooms of 2-3 people. You can respond to the questions, share a story from your life, or simply listen - whatever feels right for you.

Step 03

Share and close

Everyone returns to the main room for a short closing. Participants share one thought, question, or takeaway from the conversation before we wrap up.

Get involved

Want to facilitate Talk Time?

You do not need to be a professional teacher. As a facilitator, you help create a friendly space where Alumni members can speak, listen, and practice without pressure.

You can lead one time or on a regular basis - using ready-made materials from the Leadership Team or your own topic.

Lead Talk Time

Build communication skills

Practice leading discussions and managing group dynamics - all in English.

Add it to your resume

Facilitation is a meaningful volunteer experience you can highlight in interviews and on LinkedIn.

Use your own topic

Have something to share? Bring your own topic and lead the conversation your way.
