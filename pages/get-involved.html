Choose how you want to contribute

There are many ways to be part of Alumni Community.

You can share an idea, support one activity, or apply for a Leadership Team role when you are ready for a bigger commitment.

Getting involved is an unpaid volunteer opportunity. It can help you practice English, build confidence, develop teamwork and leadership skills, support other Alumni members, and gain experience for your résumé, LinkedIn profile, or future interviews.

You are welcome to contribute in a way that fits your life, time, and energy.

Help Alumni with your feedback, ideas, and real experience

You can help Alumni even if you do not have time to volunteer right now.

Share your feedback, suggest a Talk Time topic, recommend a helpful resource, tell us about a recent interview experience, request a topic you would like to read about on the website, or let us know if something is not working.

Your ideas help us understand what Alumni members need and what we can improve.

You can write in English or another language. We will not publish your name, email, or personal details without your permission.

Share an idea or experience

Take part in one task or community activity

If you are ready to give a little more time, you can help with a specific activity or small project.

You might facilitate a Talk Time session, support a Mock Interview session, lead a webinar or workshop, help prepare a discussion topic, contribute useful content, or bring your own idea to life.

Some activities can be one-time. Others can happen weekly or monthly, depending on your time and interest.

You will not do everything alone. The Leadership Team can help you understand the task, prepare materials, and support you along the way.

This can be a good way to practice English, build confidence, try something new, and gain experience for your résumé, LinkedIn profile, or future interviews.

Lead or support an activity

Organize and grow Alumni Community

The Leadership Team helps Alumni Community stay active, welcoming, and useful for members.

This is a volunteer role with more regular responsibility than one-time help.

Leadership Team members help organize community activities, welcome new members, support communication channels, plan events, keep information up to date, and help the community grow in a clear and realistic way.

You will not do this alone. The team works together, shares tasks, and helps new volunteers get started.

The list of open roles may change over time. You can read the current Leadership Team role descriptions and requirements here.

If you are interested in an open role, you can send your résumé. If a role you are interested in is not open right now, you can still share your interest, and we may contact you later when it becomes available.
