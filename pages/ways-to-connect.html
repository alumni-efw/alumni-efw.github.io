Stay connected in the way that works for you

After English for Work, you are welcome to stay connected with EFW Alumni Community through our private community groups.

You do not have to join every group or be active all the time.
Choose what feels useful for you now — WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or both.

Choose your space

Get updates, build connections, or both

Each space supports Alumni in a different way. An Alumni admin may message you privately if we need to confirm your name, email, English for Work course, or teacher. After we confirm your membership, we will approve access to the group or groups you requested.

After you join

What happens next

New announcements are usually shared about once a week, or when there is something important.

You do not need to do everything right away. Start with one small step that feels useful to you now:

Leave a short comment about yourself on our current LinkedIn Welcome post

Come to an Alumni Zoom event when the topic feels useful to you. Participation is flexible — join when you have time.

Send the Alumni Team a question, topic idea, or suggestion. This helps us understand what members need.

Community care

Help keep our groups useful and respectful

All members can post in our WhatsApp and LinkedIn groups.

To keep the groups helpful for everyone, please share messages connected to Alumni Community, English practice, job search, interviews, professional networking, career events, volunteer opportunities, or useful community resources.

Please avoid spam, unrelated promotions, political debates, offensive content, or someone else's private information without permission.

Before posting, you can ask yourself:

"Will this be useful or supportive for other Alumni members?"

If the answer is yes, it is probably a good fit for the group. See community guidelines
