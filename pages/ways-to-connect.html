Get updates, build connections, or do both

Each space supports Alumni in a different way. Request access to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or both.

An Alumni admin may message you privately if we need to confirm your name, email, English for Work course, or teacher.

After we confirm your membership, we will approve access to the group or groups you requested.

Community care

Help keep our groups useful and respectful

All members can post in our WhatsApp and LinkedIn groups.

To keep the groups helpful for everyone, please share messages connected to Alumni Community, English practice, job search, interviews, professional networking, career events, volunteer opportunities, or useful community resources.

Please avoid spam, unrelated promotions, political debates, offensive content, or private information about other people without permission.

Before posting, you can ask yourself:

"Will this be useful or supportive for other Alumni members?"

If the answer is yes, it is probably a good fit for the group.
