Stay connected in the way that works for you

After English for Work, you are welcome to keep in touch with Alumni Community through WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and email updates.

We use these channels to share practical Alumni information, such as Talk Time topics and session links, reminders, schedule changes, upcoming events, job fairs, career-related resources, volunteer opportunities, new website resources, and important community updates.

You do not have to join every channel or be active all the time. Choose the option that feels easiest for you — or use all of them if you want to stay more connected.

Choose your connection style

Each channel may share similar important Alumni updates, but the experience is different.
Choose the channel you are most likely to check — or join all three if you want to stay fully connected

Join WhatsApp WhatsApp group is a closed community channel for Alumni members. It is the easiest place to notice reminders before Talk Time, last-minute schedule changes, and short community updates. Members can also ask relevant questions and share helpful information. To keep the group useful and respectful, all members are expected to follow the Community Guidelines.
Join LinkedIn LinkedIn group is a professional networking space for Alumni members. It can be especially useful if you are busy, already working, or cannot attend Alumni activities regularly but still want to stay connected.
You can also use LinkedIn to connect with other Alumni members, practice sending connection requests, and build your professional network.
