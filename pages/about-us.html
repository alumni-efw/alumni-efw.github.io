A place to continue after English for Work

English for Work may end, but your journey does not have to continue alone. Alumni Community is a welcoming space for graduates to stay connected, practice English, build confidence, and support each other.

You can participate in a way that fits your life, time, and energy. Come often, come sometimes, or come back when you need encouragement.

What is the EFW Alumni Community?
We are a is a volunteer-led community for English for Work alumni.
Why Alumni

Support for the next step

Alumni Community helps graduates stay connected after the program and feel less alone during job search, interviews, work, family responsibilities, and life transitions.

Stay connected

Meet people who understand the English for Work experience and keep in touch through community channels.

Practice with confidence

Use English in friendly conversations, ask questions, share ideas, and build communication confidence over time.

Find encouragement

Return when you need useful information, support, belonging, or a reminder that you are not doing this alone.

The people behind Alumni

A community supported by alumni

Alumni Community is supported by a volunteer Leadership Team and active Alumni members.

The Leadership Team helps keep the community organized, welcoming, and useful, while members help the community grow through questions, ideas, conversation, feedback, and support.

Get involved

Organize and support the community

Helping with activities, communication channels, new member support, resources, events, and content updates.

Practice and grow

Leading events, activities, and communication gives members real experience building teamwork, confidence, and leadership skills.

Contribute when ready

Some members participate often. Others return when they have time or need support. Both are welcome, and small contributions matter.
