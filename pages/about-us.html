Your next step after English for Work

EFW Alumni Community is a volunteer-led community where English for Work graduates can stay connected, share real-life experiences, practice English, and support each other at their own pace.

You do not need to be perfect, very active, or ready to volunteer. Start with one small step: read, listen, join a conversation, ask a question, or come back when you need support.

How Alumni works

Our community principles

Alumni Community is not another required class or a job placement service. It is a resource you can return to for encouragement, practice, ideas, and people who understand your path.

Connect

We help alumni build meaningful personal and professional connections.

Share

We share useful information, experiences, resources, and ideas that may help other members move forward.

Support

We create a respectful and welcoming space where members feel valued, understood, and encouraged.

Grow

We believe people grow through practice, small steps, collaboration, and learning from each other.

Every role matters

Different stories, shared understanding

Our alumni come from different countries, languages, professional fields, and stages of life. Some are preparing for interviews, and others are building confidence in English or exploring their next step.

While our journeys are different, many of us share the experience of adapting to life and work in a new country, often while navigating career and personal transitions.

Every contribution helps strengthen our community and creates opportunities to learn and grow together.

Join events, listen and learn from others Ask questions that may benefit the whole group Share resources, experiences, and insights Help facilitate conversations and Zoom events Support fellow alumni in their journey
Volunteer-led community

Built by alumni who care

The Alumni Community is supported by alumni, speakers, facilitators, and others connected to the English for Work program who care about helping graduates continue moving forward.

People contribute in different ways—by organizing, speaking, listening, sharing ideas, or helping behind the scenes.

Thanks everyone who helps in their own way! Together, we make Alumni a place where everyone belongs.
