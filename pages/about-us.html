A place to continue your journey after English for Work

English for Work may end, but your journey does not have to continue alone.

Alumni Community is a welcoming space for English for Work graduates who want to stay connected, keep practicing English, build confidence, and support each other after the program ends.

Many of us understand what this stage can feel like. You may be looking for a job, preparing for interviews, adapting to a new professional culture, taking care of family, or trying to rebuild confidence in English. It can be exciting, but it can also feel uncertain and lonely.

Alumni Community was created to make that next step feel more supported. We believe support, respect, belonging, growth, and flexibility help people move forward.

Here, you can meet people who understand the English for Work experience, stay connected with other alumni, find useful information, practice communication, and return when you need encouragement or connection.

You do not have to participate all the time. You are welcome to be here in a way that fits your life, time, and energy.

Ways to connect

The people behind Alumni

Alumni Community is supported by a volunteer Leadership Team and active Alumni members.

The Leadership Team helps keep the community organized, welcoming, and useful. Team members help with activities, communication channels, new member support, resources, events, and website updates.

But Alumni is not only the Leadership Team.

The community grows when members ask questions, share ideas, join conversations, give feedback, support one another, or contribute in small ways. Some members participate often. Others come back when they have time or when they need support. Both are welcome.
