A place to continue after English for Work

English for Work may end, but your journey does not have to continue alone. Alumni Community is a welcoming space for graduates to stay connected, practice English, build confidence, and support each other.

You can participate in a way that fits your life, time, and energy. Come often, come sometimes, or come back when you need encouragement.

What guides us

Our community principles

Alumni Community is not another required class or obligation. It is a resource you can return to when you need connection, practice, ideas, or encouragement.

Connect

We help alumni build meaningful personal and professional connections.

Share

We share useful information, experiences, resources, and ideas that may help other members move forward.

Support

We create a respectful and welcoming space where members feel valued, understood, and encouraged.

Grow

We believe people grow through practice, small steps, collaboration, and learning from each other.

FLEXIBLE PARTICIPATION

Be part of Alumni in the way that works for you

You may be looking for a job, preparing for interviews, adapting to a new professional culture, taking care of family, or rebuilding confidence in English.

This stage can feel exciting, but it can also feel uncertain.

Alumni Community exists to make the next step feel more supported. You are welcome to participate in a way that fits your life, time, and energy.

Get involved

You can start small and choose what feels useful for you now:

Read updates and useful resources Check job postings, volunteer opportunities, and community events Join Talk Time, Mock Interview, workshops, or webinars Connect with other alumni or go to job fairs together Ask a question, share a helpful resource, or suggest an idea Support one activity or volunteer when you are ready

Your participation can be small, occasional, or more regular. It can also change over time.

You may be active for a few weeks, take a break, and come back later. That is okay.
